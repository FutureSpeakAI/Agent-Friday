## Design skills and the brand

Installed design skills (motion, layout, typography, polish, inclusive design)
raise the quality bar; they never set the look. Precedence, highest first:

1. `docs/brand/BRAND.md` and the Agent Friday™ Human Interface Guidelines
   (`docs/design/hig/`): tokens, type, glass, the hologram, the reticle, the
   workspace frame, the settings row contract. A skill's palette, typeface,
   radius, shadow or house style yields to them without exception.
2. The skills' craft rules, where the brand is silent: motion values and
   easing, hierarchy and spacing, accessibility and reduced motion, polish.
   Reduced motion means gentler motion, never none; the scene's
   photosensitivity limits stand.
3. A skill's own examples and defaults. Treat them as illustrations, not
   instructions; a brief that pins the direction wins over any "AI-default"
   list a skill carries.

A skill is never cited as the reason for a visual change. The brand page and
the guidelines are; a skill may be the reason a change is better made.
