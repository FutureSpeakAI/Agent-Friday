# The holographic desktop

The desktop of Agent Friday™ is a live 3D scene. Friday sits at the centre as a
glowing structure. Her helpers circle her as small orbs, and an ocean of dots
drifts around her. The scene is drawn by your browser on this PC.

## Friday's structures

Friday's form is one of 15 structures. The **Holographic Scene** menu lists
them in this order:

1. **GENESIS LATTICE**
2. **DYSON SPHERE**
3. **SHANNON NETWORK**
4. **GEODESIC CATHEDRAL**
5. **LOVELACE ASTROLABE**
6. **VON NEUMANN TESSERACT**
7. **DIRAC PROBABILITY**
8. **MANDELBROT SET**
9. **TURING MOBIUS**
10. **OCEAN OF LIGHT**
11. **FIBONACCI NERVE**
12. **TRANSCENDENCE**
13. **GIGA EARTH (REZ)**
14. **EINSTEIN-ROSEN BRIDGE**
15. **HAWKING RADIATION**

Every structure reacts to what Friday is doing. It changes colour and pace when
she listens, thinks and speaks.

### Choosing a structure

- **The scene pill.** The top bar shows the current structure's name with a ▾.
  Click it to open the **Holographic Scene** menu, then choose a structure.
  Friday keeps that choice.
- **Rotate structures automatically.** This menu item clears your choice.
  Friday then moves to the next structure every four days.
- **The arrow keys.** With no workspace open, **←** and **→** show the previous
  or next structure. This does not keep the choice.
- **By voice or chat.** Say "switch to the wormhole", "show the black hole" or
  "show Giga Earth". Friday keeps that choice, like the menu does. The everyday
  names "wormhole", "black hole" and "sacred sphere" also work.

## Evolution: Friday's look changes over time

Evolution makes a small change to Friday's look once a week. It is on by
default. The controls sit under **Evolution** in the **Holographic Scene**
menu.

- **Changes each week** turns weekly changes **On** or **Off**.
- **Made by** chooses who designs each change:
  - **A frontier model (Friday picks)** is the default. Friday asks a cloud
    model she can use.
  - **No model: Friday's own changes** makes the change on this PC, from a
    fixed recipe. Nothing is sent.
  - **Local:** followed by a model name appears when a local model is set up.
    The change is made on this PC.
- **Evolve now** makes the next change at once.
- **Undo** goes back to the previous look.
- **Looks** lists recent looks, each with its date and who made it. **First
  look** goes back to how Friday started. **Restore** brings back any other
  look.

Every fourth change is a calmer, simpler one. A new shape appears when the page
next loads, or while the tab is in the background. Colours ease in gently, so a
new look never flashes.

**GIGA EARTH (REZ)** follows a set track of forms instead. Each step moves it to
its next form. No model is asked and nothing is sent.

### What is sent to a cloud model

The default author needs a connected cloud model and your consent to use the
cloud. Friday sends numbers only:

- the numbers that describe the current look, and the limits of each one,
- which structure is showing,
- a week of activity counts: conversation turns, tool calls, failed tool calls,
  helpers started and local model calls.

Nothing you wrote, no names and no content are sent. Activity marked off the
record is not counted. Each look a cloud model made has a **What was sent** button that shows
the exact message.

### Without a cloud model

If no cloud model is connected, no step runs and the week does not move on.
This also happens if cloud use was never allowed, or Friday is set to keep
everything on this PC. Friday never switches to another author by herself.

The menu then says "Evolution is waiting for a cloud model", with the reason.
It offers **Connect a cloud model**, and **Use** a named local model
**instead** when one is set up. You also get one low-priority notice a week.

### A look too heavy for this PC

Friday times how smoothly the scene draws. If a new look draws more than 10%
slower than the one before, she puts the old look back and undoes the step.
She says "That look was too heavy for this computer; I went back".

### Evolution by voice

Say "Friday, evolve now", "undo that look", "go back to last month's look" or
"what changed?". "Turn evolution off" stops weekly changes.

## Helper orbs

When Friday hands work to a helper, an orb appears and circles her. An orb is
a helper, never Friday herself. At most 8 orbs show at once.

### What an orb shows

- **Kind.** Each kind of helper has its own shape: research, code, media,
  mail, scheduled, system and general helpers.
- **Colour.** Each orb takes one of five colours (teal, pink, blue, sand or
  violet), so two helpers are easy to tell apart.
- **Local or cloud.** Work on this PC shows the orb's full colour. Cloud work
  shows a paler tint and an outer ring. The label also names the model.
- **Progress.** The orb moves closer to Friday only as real progress is made.
- **Tool calls.** Each tool call gives off a small spark.
- **Its own helpers.** A helper's own helpers circle it as small moons.
- **Needs your OK.** An orb that is waiting for your approval turns amber, glows
  slowly and moves a little forward. Its label starts with "needs your OK".
- **Failed.** A failed helper turns red and its label starts with "Failed".

### Using an orb by hand

The orbs respond on the bare scene, away from windows and buttons.

- **Tap** an orb to open it. An amber orb opens its approval card. A failed
  orb opens its task page. Any other orb opens its thread of steps.
- **Hold** an orb still to pause it, and hold again to resume. This works only
  for a helper that can pause. Otherwise Friday says pausing is not possible yet.
- **Fling** an orb away from Friday to cancel it. A note says the helper "will
  stop", with **Undo** for 5 seconds. After that, the helper stops at its next
  step.
- **Drop** an orb on Friday to see its status in one line at the top of the
  screen. For example, which step it is on.
- **Drop** an orb at the edge of the screen to open its task page in its own
  browser tab. If the browser blocks the tab, the page opens in Friday instead.

### The receipt and the sky of the day

When a helper finishes well, a caption appears at the top for a few seconds. It
names the helper, what it did, the model, and how long it took. It shows the
cost when that is known. Click the caption to open the task.

Each task that finished successfully today leaves a faint star above the
scene. Tap a star to open that task. The stars clear at the start of a new day.
Work that failed or was cancelled leaves no star; find it in **Activity**.

## The ocean of dots

The dots around Friday show her state, and only hers:

- **Idle:** a slow drift past you.
- **Thinking:** a faint current flows toward her.
- **Working on this PC:** the dots nearest her glow.
- **Sending to a cloud model:** a wave of light runs through the dots. It fires
  only on a real send, never for decoration.
- **Offline:** the dots go still and the scene dims.

## Flash safety

The scene has a brightness limiter. It always runs, and there is no setting
for it. It limits how fast any large part of the screen can change brightness.
A small bright thing moving about is left alone. A fresh scene fades in from
black.

When Windows or your browser asks for reduced motion, the scene calms down:

- a structure change is a slow crossfade, with no flash, zoom or scatter,
- speech moves the scene half as much, and speaking does not speed up the spin,
- orbs do not spin or spark, and the dots stop drifting.

These protections cover the holographic scene. **Settings > Appearance > 3D
effects** sets how much the other 3D views glow and move.

## The widget

The widget is a small window that is mostly Friday. It shows her structure, a
voice button, one line of what she is saying, and anything waiting for your
decision.

- **F9** switches to the widget, and **F9** again comes back.
- **Shrink the window.** A window smaller than about 420 by 460 pixels becomes
  the widget. It goes back when you make the window bigger again.
- **`/widget`.** Open Friday at the address `/widget` to start in the widget.
  Chrome can install that address as an app window of its own.
- **⧉ Float on top of other windows.** This opens the widget as a small
  picture-in-picture window that stays above other apps. Browsers without this
  feature keep the widget in place.
- **⤢ Back to the full desktop** leaves the widget.
- **Voice.** The 🎤 button starts and stops voice, the same as on the desktop.
- **Decisions.** The first approval waiting shows its title with **APPROVE** and
  **DENY**. Some cards, such as file access, are decided only from their full
  card, so the widget does not offer them.

## View in 3D

Some workspaces have a **🧊 View in 3D** bar at the top. It shows the same
records as cards in a 3D view. **✕ Close 3D** goes back. These have it:

- **News**, **Contacts** and **Trust** (your people, by how much you trust them),
- **Messages**,
- **Calendar** (a week at a time),
- **Media**,
- the **Model browser** in **Settings > Models**.

The 3D views only read. Select a card to see its details, and open it to act on
it in the workspace. Press **?** inside a 3D view for its keys.

### Drop zones in Messages

In Messages, drag cards onto a zone, or lasso several with **Q** or Alt-drag.
A card moves only once the change is made, and bounces back if it is refused.
Every change shows **Undo (Z)**.

- **Archive**, **Star**, **Read** and **Unread** change Gmail too, for an
  account reconnected with sending. Otherwise they change Friday's view only.
- **Trash** moves the message to Gmail's Trash, which keeps it 30 days. It
  needs an account that allows it.
- **Gmail labels** add that label in Gmail. They need an account reconnected
  with sending.
- **Snooze** (until tomorrow morning) and the lanes (**Career**, **Finance**,
  **Projects**, **Family**, **Subscriptions** and **Noise**) change Friday only.
  Friday learns from a lane move.

Nothing in the 3D view deletes for good. See [Mail](mail.md) for the inbox.

### Read later in News

In News, drop a story on **Read later** to keep it, or press **S**. **Undo**
takes it back out.

### Media's 3D layout

Media's **3D** layout shows your creations folder in the 3D file browser. See
[Media](media.md).

## Head tracking, hand tracking and Big mode

Head tracking makes Friday float in front of the screen. Move to one side to
see around her, and lean in to see her larger. Hand tracking moves a cursor on
screen. Both need the camera on.

The first time you turn tracking on, the browser downloads the tracking code
from a public web address. The camera picture is read in the browser.

**Big mode** gives large targets and fewer things per screen, for driving Friday
by hand. Choose **With hand tracking** (the default), **Always** or **Never**.
While Big mode is on, anything that sends, deletes or spends needs a held pinch
or a spoken yes. Approval cards are unchanged. Say "big mode" or "big mode off"
to switch it by voice.

All of these live in **Settings > Appearance > Head and hand tracking**. See
[Settings](settings.md#appearance).

## What stays on your PC, and what can leave

The scene, the orbs, the widget and the 3D views are drawn on this PC. What can
leave:

- the numbers for an Evolution step, when a cloud model makes it,
- the tracking code download, the first time you turn tracking on,
- Gmail changes from the Messages drop zones, for accounts that allow them.

Approvals still work as everywhere else. See
[Approvals and receipts](approvals-and-receipts.md).
