What "Make a podcast of it" means per medium

Document, page, post
The text is the source; the hosts read it and cite it by section.
Chart, dataset, sheet
Data mode: the numbers are computed first and the hosts may only say computed numbers.
Image, video, 3D
The prompt, the credential and the description Friday wrote are the sources; the hosts say what was made and how, never what the picture "looks like" beyond its description.
Podcast, audio, music
Not offered: an episode about an episode is noise. Edit the episode instead.
Codebase
The README, the brief and the last run are the sources.
