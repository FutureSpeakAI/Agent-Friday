Agent Friday™ Human Interface Guidelines

Three layers, one system

Clickable prototypes for the surface reorganisation, built to the HIG. They borrow the conventions of professional software and keep Friday's own look: only the generated brand tokens name a colour, a face or a size. Everything in them is fictional. Press CtrlK on any page.

Layer 1 · visionOS is the bar

The holographic desktop

Eight controls in the bar, telemetry behind the connection light, a dock of three groups, the landing cluster with his countdowns and their reasons, a window with OS controls in its title bar and the workspace's tools in its header, and one approval card summoned by one amber pill.
Layer 2 · Linear and Superhuman are the bar

The workspace frame

One template applied to two real workspaces, Messages and Calendar: header with a plain status line, four toolbar actions and ⋯, a sidebar of sections, list-and-detail or calendar content, an optional inspector, the three states, list keys, and the same code at 1600px, 1000px and 640px.
Layer 3 · Claude.ai and ChatGPT settings are the bar

Settings

Thirteen pages in four groups plus About. Search that jumps to a row, or a sentence that becomes a diff and a yes. Every row has four lines: meaning, consequence, who changed it and when, undo. Status blocks are tagged status; danger zones come last.
