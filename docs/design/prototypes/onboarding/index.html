First run to first month

Seven clickable screens for the spec. They are drawings that move, not product code: nothing here talks to a server, and every number is illustrative. Keyboard works on every screen; the "Friday says" line is also an aria-live region.

  1. Minute 0

    Download one signed file. Preflight with no model. Install in under two minutes. Upgrade variant shows the vault-passphrase guarantee and rollback.

    Installer
  2. Minute 3

    She is born. Name her, see her mark form, give her an address, lock the vault, run the restore drill, choose where she thinks, how she talks, how quiet she is.

    Naming and birth
  3. Minute 12

    Connect your life: grouped cards, read and write separate, what she sees and where, the honest Gmail note, imports that need no account.

    Connect
  4. Minute 14

    The first major task begins: the graph grows on screen, sources report honest time, and "what I see so far" arrives with five modest things.

    Knowledge growing
  5. Minute 20

    The review queue: twelve small questions by voice, four minutes, keys Y N U S. Merges, commitments, vault moves, page approvals.

    Review queue
  6. Day 3

    The control room: plain-language settings, voice-editable with a shown diff, modes, four lines on every setting, "why did you do that".

    Control room
  7. Any day

    The privacy map: every source, where its data flows, and the ledger of what left this machine today.

    Privacy map

Brand: black stage, cyan accent, Orbitron labels, Inter body, JetBrains Mono addresses, the Genesis Lattice as her mark. Motion only on a real event (speaking, working); none under reduced motion.
