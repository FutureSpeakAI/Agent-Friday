The three workspaces that fold in

Draft becomes a card kind

  • Keeps: write in your voice for any channel; send to clipboard or a mail draft.
  • Gains: drafts persist as cards with status, sources and versions; they appear on Today and In progress; "turn this into…".
  • Loses: nothing: today a draft is not saved anywhere once it is sent.

Content becomes the Pipeline and the Calendar

  • Keeps: compose per platform, hold for review, queue, publish, analytics.
  • Gains: posts sit beside what they were made from; one approval card for every publish; the calendar shows posts with everything else that goes out.
  • Moves: connected accounts and best-times go to Settings → Accounts; analytics becomes a card's "after it went out" panel.

Studio becomes Create tools and card kinds

  • Keeps: images, video, music, timelines, podcasts from any sources, the 3D file browser.
  • Gains: every creation is a card with credentials and privacy; search across everything; "turn this into…".
  • Moves: the 3D browser becomes a layout of the Library plus "Browse this PC"; the production pipeline becomes a project template.
