Media: one home for everything she makes with you

Six clickable screens for the spec. They are drawings that move, not product code: nothing here talks to a server, every card and date is illustrative, and the names are synthetic. The same twenty cards feed every screen. Keyboard works everywhere. The top bar and chat tray are the unified shell's, drawn to its rules.

  1. Library

    One card per piece of work: type, status, provenance, privacy, where it is published. Default views (Today, In progress, Needs you, Published), projects, types and search, so it is never a junk drawer.

    Library
  2. Pipeline

    The same cards by status: Idea, Draft, In review, Scheduled, Published. Drag to advance; Scheduled asks when, Published always asks you first. This is where Drafts and Content went.

    Pipeline
  3. Calendar

    Only cards with a time: what went out, what is due to go out, and what needs you by when. Two weeks at a glance; drag to reschedule.

    Calendar
  4. Card

    One editor frame for every medium, with the status strip, provenance, privacy and "turn this into…": a draft into a podcast, an article into slides, a chart into a data podcast.

    Card and editor
  5. Own tab

    Any card, or the whole workspace, in its own Chrome tab or a floating window, wearing the shell's one top bar, with a live measure of the frame.

    Own tab
  6. Boundaries

    News stays reading, the Salon stays the IDE, Media is making. What Draft, Content and Studio keep, gain and lose when they fold in.

    Media, News, Salon

Brand: the --fr-* tokens from BRAND.md; Inter for text, Orbitron for labels only, JetBrains Mono for data; amber only where something needs you; status always a word, never a colour alone. Agent Friday™ by FutureSpeak.AI™.
