# Brand fidelity audit: every workspace, in a window and in a tab

> **Status:** first pass of P-BRAND-FIDELITY, built on `feat/unified-shell` as piece 4 of
> [unified-shell.md](../design/active/unified-shell.md) §6; K2 fixed by its piece 10 (§12).
> Each finding below says what this pass did about it, or why it is left and where it goes
> next.
> **Last verified:** 2026-09-30, against the tree of this branch.
> **Rules it applies:** [BRAND.md](BRAND.md), including the section on workspaces and
> controls that this pass adds.

## Method

Every workspace in `static/workspace_registry.js` that can have a tab (18 of 19; Settings
is a panel) was captured at one viewport, 1600 by 1000, in both contexts: as a desktop
window opened by `/?workspace=<id>`, and as its own tab at `/w/<id>`. With the start
screen that is 37 frames, taken one browser at a time against the running server with
every write stubbed, and each frame was looked at. The captures stay on the owner's
machine: they show the owner's mail, calendar, people and health records.

Each frame was also measured in the page: the document title and icon links, the
workspace heading's face, size and ink, the face and ink of every text node, the style
signature of every button, lines that describe an empty, loading or error state, the
voice and settings controls, and the controls in the window's title bar or the tab's bar.
The frames were taken again after the fixes and compared.

The bars: Linear and Superhuman for consistency (one control, one look, one place,
everything reachable from the keyboard), and the brand check in BRAND.md.

## Left by rule

- The avatar and the scene belong to the avatar session: the scene's fallback line ("That
  look was too heavy for this computer; I went back") and its state chip are theirs. The two
  Orbitron declarations without a fallback in the Evolution controls now read
  `'Orbitron, sans-serif'` (`5352ca12`): the brand guard arrives with this branch, so this
  branch keeps it passing.
- `ui_parts/app.html` still lacks parts of `index.html` (QuickSwitch, the orbs control, the
  chat stream, the Settings Updates section). The fixes above are mirrored where the mirror
  has the code.
