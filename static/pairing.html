What happens next

On the new device, open Friday and start pairing. Six digits appear on the device and a card appears here. Press Approve on this screen only if the digits match and the device is yours; press Decline if you did not start it. Pairing closes by itself when the time is up, and closing it ends any pairing not yet approved.

Anyone on your home network can ask to pair while it is open, which is why it stays open for minutes, not hours.
