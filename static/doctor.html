Doctor

A check of Friday on this PC: versions, the state of each part, error signatures, settings drift and speed. It contains no conversations, memories, file names, addresses or vault content. Friday never sends it anywhere by itself. To share it, save the file and attach it yourself, or choose below to be asked each week whether to email it to FutureSpeak.

Diagnostic start

To find out why Friday will not start or misbehaves, choose a diagnostic start below and restart Friday, or run friday diagnose. That one start is in safe mode, records how far the start got and when, and saves a Doctor report with it in the diagnostics folder. It holds no content and is never sent. The start after it is as it would have been.

      After a restore
      
  
      
  
        
  
        Check that your services are connected again (Settings, Connect your services), then let Friday act again on this PC. Resume waits until this PC holds Friday's governance key and every channel and the phone line, where switched on, is connected again or switched off.
        
  
  
  
        

        


        
  
        Backup: Friday Bundle
        
  
        One encrypted file with your memory, vault, settings, skills, workspaces, receipts and conversations. Service sign-ins are not included; you reconnect them after a restore. It is saved in Documents, Friday Bundles. Without the passphrase it cannot be opened, by anyone.
        
  
  
        
  
        
  
  
        
  
        To restore on another PC: install Friday, do not set it up, then run friday restore <file>.
        

        


        
  
        Your data as plain files
        
  
        Writes everything Friday keeps as files you can open without Friday: wiki pages and memories as Markdown, every conversation, your goals, every receipt with whether the record verifies, contacts (also as a spreadsheet) and settings with every secret blanked. Passwords, keys and tokens are never included. The folder goes to Documents, Friday Exports. It is not encrypted: keep it somewhere safe.
        
  
  
        

        


        
  
        Scheduled bundles
        
  
        
    
        No backup is set up yet. Everything Friday knows is in one folder on this PC. Choose a folder below (another drive or a network share is best), a passphrase, and turn scheduled bundles on.
        
    
    
  
        
  
        Friday can make a bundle every day or every week into a folder you choose (this PC or a network share), check each one after it is written, keep the newest few, and now and then rehearse a restore from the newest one without changing anything. The passphrase for scheduled bundles, with the folder and how many to keep, is kept in this account's credential store (Windows Credential Manager) so they can be made while you are away: anyone who can open this account can open them. On a system without a credential store, scheduled bundles cannot be turned on.

        Revoke all action authority
        
  
        Take back everything Friday may do on your behalf, at once: every waiting approval is withdrawn, every standing permission (pre-approved sends, file and folder grants, desktop control, websites she may act on) is taken back, scheduled jobs are paused and remote channels stopped. Until you restore it, nothing leaves this PC and nothing is read at your connected services. Restoring lifts the hold only; you give permissions back one by one.
